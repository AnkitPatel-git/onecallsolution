Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Onecall Solution (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through onecallsolution.in.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry form or email us, we may collect your name, company, phone number, email address and message content. Server logs may record IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and diagnostics.

How we use information

We use enquiry details solely to respond to your request, prepare proposals, and — where relevant — complete vendor onboarding. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We may share information with subprocessors who help us operate email/form delivery (for example form delivery providers) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Retention

Enquiry records are retained as needed for sales follow-up and legal/accounting obligations, then deleted or archived according to internal policy.

Your choices

To access, correct or request deletion of your enquiry data, email info@onecallsolution.in.
